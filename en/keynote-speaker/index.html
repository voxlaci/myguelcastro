Leadership through music: talks on discipline and performance.

Myguel Santos e Castro is a keynote speaker with direct language and practical examples drawn from thirty years on stage as a conductor. His talks cover leadership, discipline, resilience and performance — with or without a live musical demonstration, in English or Portuguese.

Request availability WhatsApp

Topics

  • Leadership through music — what a team or a choir teaches about trust, listening and alignment.
  • Discipline and resilience — thirty years of practice, rehearsal and stage, applied to any field that demands rigour.
  • Presence and performance — how to occupy the stage (literal or figurative) with confidence.
  • Personal development — real stories from a path that started at age seven and crossed Angola, Boston and Lisbon.

Frequently asked questions

What topics does he speak on?

Leadership, discipline, resilience, performance, stage presence and personal development, with practical examples from his conducting career.

Do the talks include live music?

They can — from a spoken talk to a keynote with musical demonstration or a short audience exercise.

Does he speak in English and Portuguese?

Yes, in both languages.

Company conferences and conventions, schools, universities and team leadership sessions.
