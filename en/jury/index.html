Jury · Artistic Direction

Choral competition jury and artistic direction for festivals.

Myguel Santos e Castro serves as jury for choral competitions and auditions, and as artistic director for festivals and cultural projects, with a rigorous, sensitive assessment oriented towards participants' growth — in Portugal and internationally.

What I do

  • Choral competition jury — technical and artistic assessment at competitive competitions and festivals.
  • Audition jury — selecting singers and ensembles for projects and institutions.
  • Artistic direction — curation and programming for festivals and cultural projects.
  • One-off artistic consulting — an experienced outside perspective for choral projects in development.

Background

Artistic director of the Ramos Palm Sunday Festival since its creation — an annual international choral festival in Cascais, with guest conductors and choirs from several countries. He has served as jury in workshops, masterclasses and competitions across several countries, combining technical rigour with human sensitivity.
