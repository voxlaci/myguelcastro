Guest conductor

Choral and orchestral conducting, in Portugal and internationally.

Myguel Santos e Castro has conducted choirs and orchestras at festivals, concerts and intensive choral projects for over 30 years — in Portugal and in countries including the Netherlands, Japan, Estonia, Argentina, the United Kingdom, France, Italy, Senegal and Brazil.

What I do

  • Concert and festival conducting — choral and orchestral repertoire, with guest choirs, soloists and orchestra.
  • Intensive choral projects — short residencies and rehearsals with large groups, prepared for a concert or festival.
  • Guest conducting — one-off conducting for other institutions' choirs and orchestras.
  • Choral conducting masterclasses — for conductors, teachers and artists, including within the Ramos Palm Sunday Festival.
  • Jury and artistic direction — competitions, auditions and cultural projects.

Background

Founder and artistic director of VoxLaci since 1996, and artistic director of the Ramos Palm Sunday Festival — an annual international choral festival in Cascais, with guest conductors and choirs from several countries. He has conducted large-scale choral projects, including the GRATIAS Requiem concert, and his work has reached audiences through radio, television and cinema.

Musical training since age seven (piano, guitar, music theory); by seventeen, teaching music in over twenty schools in Portugal; holds a degree in Management and Public Administration from ISCSP, University of Lisbon.

Why invite him

Thirty years on stage, leading projects with hundreds of singers in real-world contexts, and an approach that combines musical rigour with genuine attention to the group. The same experience behind the Ramos Palm Sunday Festival — masterclasses, intensive rehearsals and concerts with international conductors and choirs — is available to other choirs, orchestras and festivals.
