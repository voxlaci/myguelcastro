Artistic Consulting

An experienced outside perspective for choirs, festivals and cultural projects.

Myguel Santos e Castro provides artistic consulting to choirs, festivals and cultural institutions looking to develop — drawing on his experience founding and leading VoxLaci since 1996 and serving as artistic director of the Ramos Palm Sunday Festival.

Request consulting WhatsApp

What I do

  • Choral project diagnosis — repertoire, section organisation, vocal development and group dynamics.
  • Programme and season structuring — planning repertoire and calendar across a season.
  • Festival and institution guidance — an experienced outside perspective for projects in development.
  • One-off consulting — one or a few focused sessions on a specific question, with no long-term commitment.

Background

Founder and artistic director of VoxLaci since 1996 — a choral community with around twelve ensembles, for all ages and levels — and artistic director of the Ramos Palm Sunday Festival, an annual international choral festival in Cascais. Thirty years of building, adjusting and growing choral projects bring a practical, not just theoretical, standard to this consulting work.

Frequently asked questions

What kind of artistic consulting do you offer?

Diagnosing choral projects, structuring programmes and seasons, and one-off guidance for festivals and cultural institutions.

Do you work with amateur or professional choirs?

Both — the experience of leading VoxLaci applies to projects of all levels.

Is it in person or remote?

Depends on the project: in-person session, a visit to a rehearsal or festival, or remote guidance.

How do I request consulting?

Via the contact form or WhatsApp, describing the project and support needed. Response within 24-48 business hours.
